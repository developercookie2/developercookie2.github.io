Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers DeveloperCookie Publisher ("the tool"), the internal tool DeveloperCookie ("we", "us") uses to post our own game videos to our own social media accounts and to read the stats of those posts.

Who uses the tool

Only DeveloperCookie uses the tool, and only with accounts owned by DeveloperCookie. It is not offered to the public and does not access other people's accounts.

What data the tool accesses

When we connect one of our accounts through the platform's official login (Google/YouTube or TikTok), the tool may access:

  • Basic account information: the account's display name, username, profile picture and ID, used to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Our own videos and their stats: titles, links, publish dates, and view, like, comment and share counts, used to report how our posts perform.
  • Upload permission: used only to publish videos we choose, with captions and privacy settings we set.

The tool does not read direct messages, follower lists or any data about other users.

How data is stored

Login tokens and post statistics are stored only on the studio's own computer. We do not run a public server for this tool and do not store the data in any third-party database. The tool and this website do not use cookies or tracking, and do not store anything on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. It is used only by DeveloperCookie to manage and measure our own posts.

Google API Services

DeveloperCookie Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time: for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for TikTok, in the app under Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions. When access is removed we delete the stored login tokens. Stored post statistics are deleted on request.
