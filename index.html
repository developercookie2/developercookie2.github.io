DeveloperCookie Publisher

DeveloperCookie Publisher is the studio's internal tool for sharing our own game videos. It lets the studio:

  • sign in to the studio's own YouTube and TikTok accounts with the platforms' official login,
  • upload our game trailers and clips to those accounts with a title, caption and privacy setting chosen by us,
  • read the view, like and comment counts of our own posts, so we can see which clips work best.

The tool is used only by the studio, on the studio's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
